
# Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

## Given

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3\\
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0\\
-2 & 3\\
4 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## 1. Matrix sizes

Matrix $A$ has 2 rows and 3 columns.

$$
\boxed{\dim(A)=2\times3}
$$

Matrix $B$ has 3 rows and 2 columns.

$$
\boxed{\dim(B)=3\times2}
$$

## 2. Matrix entries

$$
\boxed{a_{12}=-1}
$$

$$
\boxed{a_{23}=5}
$$

$$
\boxed{b_{21}=-2}
$$

$$
\boxed{b_{32}=1}
$$

## 3. Row and column vectors

Second row of $A$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix}
$$

First column of $B$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1\\
-2\\
4
\end{pmatrix}
$$
